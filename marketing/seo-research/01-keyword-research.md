# DTailWash — Core/Head Keyword Research (EN + ES)
**Market:** Miami-Dade County, FL | **Date:** 2026-07-07 | **Scope:** Core/head + mid-tail commercial & transactional terms (customer + contractor-recruitment demand)

## Data Source Labels (read this first)
- **Measured (SERP composition):** Live Brave Search results pulled for 3 seed queries — shows *who* ranks today, used to infer difficulty tier.
- **Measured (Autocomplete demand):** Google Autocomplete harvested via `suggest.py --expand` (27-seed alphabet sweep) for 6 terms + 9 single-shot pulls = 21 live pulls, ~1,000 total suggestion strings. Suggestion *count/density* is a real, current demand signal (more completions = more real query variation happening); it is **not** a search-volume number.
- **Estimated (volume):** No DataForSEO/Ahrefs/GSC connection was available this session (checked; not installed). All monthly-volume figures below are modeled: national/category volume benchmarks (public industry knowledge) × Miami-Dade population share (~2.7M / ~335M US ≈ 0.8%, rounded to a 1–1.5% local-intent multiplier because "detailing" + "car wash" skew warmer in a car-dependent, high-net-worth-density metro) × autocomplete-density cross-check. Treat every volume figure as an **order-of-magnitude planning number**, not a committed forecast. Get a paid tool (Ahrefs/Semrush/DataForSEO, ~$99–200/mo) before setting hard content-team KPIs on these numbers.
- **Estimated (difficulty):** Model inference (1–100) from SERP composition + domain types observed (independent local shops vs. franchise vs. UGC/aggregator).
- **N/A:** Marked explicitly where autocomplete returned 0 signal — do not read as "zero demand," read as "unvalidated, test before investing."

## Executive Summary
1. Brave SERP sampling for `mobile car detailing miami` (Measured) shows **zero national aggregator or franchise in the top 10** — it's 9 independent local operators + 1 Yelp listing. This is a low-authority-bar SERP: a well-built, address-rich, review-heavy DTailWash page can realistically compete without a big backlink budget.
2. `ceramic coating miami` (Measured SERP) is more contested: Ceramic Pro (national franchise, dealer-page network) plus 7 well-optimized independent dedicated landing pages plus Yelp. Estimated Difficulty 55 — winnable, but needs a dedicated dealer-page-style ceramic-coating page and Miami-specific proof (salt-air/UV durability angle, which 2 competitors already use).
3. **Spanish is the single biggest gap found.** SERP for `detallado de autos a domicilio miami` (Measured) includes a Facebook group post and a TikTok discover page ranking on page 1 — that only happens when organic competition is thin. Only 3 dedicated small-business sites exist. Estimated Difficulty 20–25 vs. 35–55 for English equivalents.
4. Google Autocomplete literally surfaces **"car detailing kendall"** as a top completion under the bare seed `car detailing near me` (Measured) — Google already associates this generic head term with a specific Miami-Dade neighborhood, confirming hyper-local intent is real and crawlable.
5. `car detailing jobs near me` beat the bare term `car detailing jobs` as the #1 autocomplete completion out of 225 total variants harvested (Measured) — contractor-recruitment "near me" intent is at least as strong a pattern as customer-side "near me" intent. The contractor-acquisition funnel is currently un-served by any Miami-specific page.
6. Spanish contractor-recruitment phrasing is **unvalidated**: `trabajo de detallado de autos` and `como ser detailer de autos` returned **0** autocomplete completions (N/A). Do not build a page assuming this exact phrase has volume — test `trabajo lavando carros`, `empleo lavado de autos a domicilio` instead (see Next Steps).
7. `lavado de autos a domicilio` (no city qualifier) returned 132 autocomplete completions (Measured) — one of the densest results in this research — but the completions are dominated by Latin American cities (CDMX, Monterrey, Bogotá, Santiago, San Juan/Bayamón). Read this as strong **category-level demand for the "a domicilio" format** across the Spanish-speaking world, not as Miami-specific volume; the Miami-qualified estimate is deliberately conservative (Estimated 40–90/mo) until localized data exists.
8. Highest single **Opportunity score** in English: `mobile car detailing miami` (Opportunity 9.0). Highest in Spanish: `detallado de autos a domicilio miami` (Opportunity 12.0) — lower estimated volume but half the difficulty of the English equivalent.

## Quick Wins (Low Difficulty + High Intent — build first)
1. **`detallado de autos a domicilio miami`** — Est. vol 65, Difficulty 25, Opportunity 12.0. Facebook/TikTok on page 1 of the live SERP = the weakest competitive field found in this entire research pass.
2. **`car detailing hialeah`** — Est. vol 20, Difficulty 20. Only 7 live autocomplete completions and a SERP full of generic "car wash," not "detailing" — a dedicated page can own the term outright.
3. **`lavado de autos a domicilio precios`** — Est. vol 25, Difficulty 20. It's the #1 completion of its seed and zero ES competitors publish pricing — a bilingual price table closes this instantly.
4. **`car detailing doral` / `car detailing homestead`** — Est. vol 15–18 each, Difficulty 20. Both cities have thin, non-detailing-specific SERPs; cheap to spin up as city pages once the Kendall/Miami Beach templates exist.
5. **`mobile detailing job description`** — Est. vol 12, Difficulty 20. Zero real competition; doubles as contractor-recruitment GEO content.

## Growth Targets (Higher Volume, Medium Difficulty — build after Quick Wins)
1. **`car detailing near me`** — Est. vol 380, Difficulty 60. The anchor head term; win requires Google Business Profile optimization + local-pack signals in addition to on-page content, since 255 autocomplete completions show intent is broad and largely local-pack-driven, not purely organic.
2. **`ceramic coating miami`** — Est. vol 95, Difficulty 55. Requires a dedicated dealer-style landing page to compete with the Ceramic Pro franchise node and 7 optimized independents already in the SERP.
3. **`car detailing jobs near me`** — Est. vol 60, Difficulty 50. Highest-volume contractor-recruitment term found (#1 of 225 completions); competes with Indeed/ZipRecruiter-style aggregators, so needs a genuinely useful contractor-facing page (pay structure, service-area map, apply flow) rather than a thin jobs listing.

- **"car detailing near me vs car wash near me"** — several autocomplete completions blur "detailing" and "wash"; a short comparison/definition block ("What's the difference between a car wash and full detailing?") captures both the informational and the miscategorized transactional traffic.
